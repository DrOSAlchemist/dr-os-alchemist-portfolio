# DrOSAlchemist Brand Identity

## Idea

DrOSAlchemist is the systems engineer who joins infrastructure disciplines into one understandable operating picture. The visual language combines a precise engineering grid with a small amount of signal color: composed, curious, and grounded in real operations.

## Mark and Wordmark

The mark is two crossing signal paths meeting at a catalyst point. The crossing paths stand for infrastructure boundaries: model and compute, service and network, automation and human judgment. The center point is where a small, deliberate change transforms the system.

Use the full wordmark `DrOSAlchemist` in the site header. Use the mark by itself only in compact contexts such as the footer and browser icon. Preserve the original proportions and keep a clear margin equal to the center-point diameter.

## Color

- `#142421` — deep ink, primary text and dark system panels
- `#EAF0ED` — cool field-paper background
- `#F7FAF7` — raised surfaces and diagram nodes
- `#D5EC74` — catalyst lime, active highlights
- `#5270DD` — signal blue, navigation and links
- `#F27B5E` — warning coral, small emphasis points

Use lime, blue and coral as distinct signals. Keep body copy on the light field background or deep ink panels for contrast.

## Typography

- Space Grotesk Variable for display and interface text
- IBM Plex Mono for labels, metadata and system annotations
- Use short uppercase mono labels for technical context; do not set long paragraphs in the mono face

## Voice

Be direct, thoughtful and specific. Prefer operational language over hype. Explain the constraint, the design choice and how success is measured. The portfolio speaks in first person; technical articles distinguish tested guidance from context-dependent recommendations.
